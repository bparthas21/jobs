
# Senior AI Architect Job Description

Job Description: Senior AI Architect

Industry: Banking & Financial Services
Experience: 12+ years in IT, including 5+ years in AI/ML architecture
Role Level: Senior / Lead Architect

Position Overview

We are seeking an experienced Senior AI Architect to lead the design, development, and implementation of enterprise-scale Artificial Intelligence (AI) solutions for a leading banking organization. The ideal candidate will have deep expertise in Generative AI, Agentic AI, Large Language Models (LLMs), Machine Learning, and cloud-native AI architectures, with a strong understanding of banking systems, enterprise security, data governance, and regulatory compliance.

The role requires close collaboration with business stakeholders, enterprise architects, data scientists, engineering teams, cybersecurity teams, and risk management functions to deliver secure, scalable, and responsible AI solutions.

Key Responsibilities

Enterprise AI Architecture: Define and implement enterprise AI architecture, reference patterns, technology standards, and integration strategies aligned with banking business objectives.

Generative AI & Agentic AI: Architect GenAI solutions leveraging LLMs, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), AI agents, multi-agent orchestration, and enterprise knowledge platforms.

AI/ML Engineering: Design scalable machine learning pipelines, model deployment architectures, inference services, and MLOps/LLMOps frameworks.

Cloud & Hybrid Architecture: Architect AI solutions across AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, and hybrid/on-premises environments, ensuring scalability, reliability, and cost optimization.

Banking Systems Integration: Integrate AI capabilities with core banking platforms, payment systems, fraud detection, risk management, customer service, and enterprise data platforms.

AI Security & Governance: Implement AI security controls, model risk management, data privacy, explainability, auditability, and responsible AI practices aligned with banking regulatory expectations.

Data Architecture: Design data ingestion, vector databases, embedding pipelines, data governance, and secure enterprise data access patterns.

Technology Leadership: Evaluate emerging AI technologies, lead architecture reviews, establish reusable design patterns, and provide technical guidance to engineering teams.

Production AI Operations: Establish monitoring, model evaluation, observability, performance optimization, drift detection, and operational resilience for production AI systems.

Required Qualifications

Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Engineering, or a related discipline.

12+ years of enterprise IT experience, with at least 5 years in AI/ML architecture, engineering, or related technical leadership.

Strong hands-on experience with Python, PyTorch, TensorFlow, Scikit-learn, and enterprise AI development frameworks.

Expertise in LLMs, RAG, prompt engineering, fine-tuning, embeddings, vector databases, and AI agent frameworks such as LangChain, LangGraph, Semantic Kernel, or equivalent technologies.

Experience with AI services and platforms such as Azure AI Foundry, Azure OpenAI, AWS Bedrock, Amazon SageMaker, or Google Vertex AI.

Strong understanding of microservices, REST APIs, event-driven architectures, Kubernetes, Docker, and CI/CD pipelines.

Experience with MLOps/LLMOps, model lifecycle management, model registries, automated evaluations, and production monitoring.

Strong knowledge of enterprise cybersecurity, IAM, encryption, data protection, and secure AI architecture.

Familiarity with banking regulations and risk management expectations, including SR 11-7, GLBA, FFIEC guidance, and applicable privacy and AI governance requirements.

Excellent communication, stakeholder management, architecture documentation, and technical leadership skills.

Preferred Qualifications

Prior experience architecting enterprise AI solutions within a large bank, financial institution, or highly regulated environment.

Experience with fraud detection, AML/KYC, credit risk, financial crime analytics, or intelligent banking operations.

Knowledge of NIST AI Risk Management Framework, ISO/IEC 42001, model governance, and Responsible AI.

Experience implementing secure enterprise GenAI platforms, AI gateways, guardrails, human-in-the-loop workflows, and agentic AI governance.

Familiarity with enterprise architecture frameworks such as TOGAF and financial services technology modernization.

Relevant certifications in AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, AI/ML, or enterprise architecture.

Expected Deliverables

Enterprise AI architecture blueprints and reference architectures.

Scalable, secure GenAI and Agentic AI solution designs.

AI platform integration and deployment standards.

AI governance, security, and model risk control frameworks.

Production-ready AI solutions with measurable business outcomes.
